FAQ: Understanding Your Rights

1. What does "partial denial" mean?
Standard FOIA term regarding withheld documents, not a rejection of your status.

2. Is there an appeal process?
Consult a legal professional and utilize the CIS Ombudsman case review (6 U.S.C. § 272).

3. Consequences of ineligibility?
An ineligibility finding complicates future visa applications. Challenge it via CIS Ombudsman.

4. Data sharing clause?
The USRAP confidentiality notice permits sharing info with foreign governments post-denial.

5. How to lodge a complaint?
Use CIS Ombudsman, DHS OIG, or a Privacy Act request to USCIS.

6. CIS Ombudsman authority?
They investigate systemic patterns of problems under 6 U.S.C. § 272.

7. Appeal for ineligibility?
No formal appeal, but use Ombudsman review and FOIA requests to document your case.

8. FOIA timelines?
20 business days, though delays are frequent. Silence is a violation.

9. If USCIS ignores FOIA?
Appeal to FOIA Appeals Office or file a complaint with DHS OIG.

10. CWS/RSC authority?
Contractors with no independent authority; they must comply with the Privacy Act.

Gereelde Vrae: Verstaan Jou Regte

1. Wat beteken "gedeeltelike weiering"?
Standaard FOIA-term vir weerhoude dokumente, nie 'n afkeuring nie.

2. Is daar 'n appèlproses?
Nee, gebruik die CIS Ombudsman-saakhersiening kragtens 6 U.S.C. § 272.

3. Gevolge van ongeskiktheid?
Dit kompliseer toekomstige aansoeke; daag dit uit op rekord.

4. Data-deling klousule?
Die kennisgewing laat deling met buitelandse regerings toe na afkeuring.

5. Hoe dien ek 'n klagte in?
Gebruik CIS Ombudsman, DHS OIG, of Privacy Act-versoeke.

6. CIS Ombudsman se gesag?
Hulle ondersoek sistemiese patrone kragtens 6 U.S.C. § 272.

7. Appèl vir ongeskiktheid?
Geen formele appèl; gebruik Ombudsman-hersiening en FOIA-rekords.

8. FOIA tydlyne?
20 werksdae, maar vertragings is algemeen. Stilte is onwettig.

9. As USCIS FOIA ignoreer?
Appelleer by FOIA-kantoor of dien klagte in by DHS OIG.

10. CWS/RSC se gesag?
Hulle is kontrakteurs sonder onafhanklike gesag oor jou data.
