The ARAI Forensic Audit is an independent, ongoing investigation into the institutional collapse of the USRAP P2 pipeline. We are documenting the systematic weaponization of administrative security queues against Afrikaners as a Particular Social Group (PSG). Whether individual applicants participate or withdraw, the record of institutional malpractice is being built. We are documenting the failure of federal due process regardless of individual attrition. If you have been denied without substantive reasoning, your silence is already part of the evidence of a failed system.
