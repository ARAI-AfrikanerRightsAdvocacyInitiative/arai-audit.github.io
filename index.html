FAQ: Understanding Your FOIA Response & Your Rights

1. What does "partial denial" mean?

"Partial denial" is a standard FOIA term. It means that some of the requested records or parts of a record were withheld under one of the nine exemptions in the Freedom of Information Act. It is not a decision on your refugee application.

2. Does this mean my case was partially rejected?

No. The denial refers only to the release of documents in response to your FOIA request. Your immigration or refugee case status is separate from the FOIA process.

3. What are the long-term consequences of an ineligibility letter?

An ineligibility finding in the USRAP is not automatically a global prohibition on future applications. Other countries make their own determinations under their own laws. However, an unchallenged ineligibility can complicate future visa or refugee applications. ARAI strongly advises every applicant to file a CIS Ombudsman case review and to document the decision as part of the systemic complaint. A formal challenge on the record is the most effective defence.

4. The confidentiality notice permits sharing with foreign governments – what does this mean in practice?

The USRAP Notice on Confidentiality (August 2024) explicitly states that if your application is denied, your information may be shared with foreign governments for the purpose of relocation to another country. It also states that the notice creates no legally enforceable rights. This is a serious privacy concern, and ARAI is documenting the scope of this clause as part of its systemic complaint.

5. How do I lodge a formal complaint about the handling of my case?

You have several channels: (1) The CIS Ombudsman – file a case review request at dhs.gov; (2) DHS OIG – file a complaint about fraud, waste, abuse, or misconduct at oig.dhs.gov; (3) A Privacy Act request to USCIS if you believe your data was mishandled. ARAI has templates for each of these.

6. What is the CIS Ombudsman and what authority do they have?

The CIS Ombudsman is an independent DHS office that assists individuals in resolving problems with USCIS. They cannot overturn a decision, but they can compel USCIS to re-examine a case, correct errors, and provide answers. Under 6 U.S.C. § 272, they are authorised to investigate systemic patterns of problems – which is precisely what ARAI is asking them to do.

7. Is there an appeal process for an ineligibility letter?

In most USRAP cases, there is no formal appeal process. However, you can: (1) request a CIS Ombudsman case review; (2) file a FOIA request for the complete record; (3) consult an immigration attorney about any possible motion to reopen; and (4) document the denial as part of ARAI's systemic pattern complaint. Every documented case strengthens the collective case.

8. How long does a FOIA request take?

USCIS has 20 business days to respond to a FOIA request, but delays are common and some requests take months. A "partial denial" means that some records were withheld under a legal exemption. ARAI provides a standard FOIA template for applicants.

9. What if USCIS ignores my FOIA request entirely?

If USCIS fails to respond within the statutory deadline, you can: (1) appeal to the USCIS FOIA Appeals Office; (2) file a lawsuit in federal district court; (3) file a complaint with the DHS OIG. Silence is not a lawful response – it is a violation of the FOIA statute, and ARAI is documenting these failures.

10. What are CWS/RSC and what authority do they have over my data?

CWS/RSC (Resettlement Support Centers) are contractors that process USRAP cases on behalf of the U.S. government. They must comply with the Privacy Act and USCIS data-use agreements. They do not have independent authority over your data. If you believe your data was misused or shared improperly, you can file a Privacy Act request with USCIS and a complaint with DHS OIG.

Gereelde Vrae: Verstaan Jou FOIA-reaksie & Jou Regte

1. Wat beteken "gedeeltelike weiering" (partial denial)?

"Partial denial" is `n standaard FOIA-term. Dit beteken dat sommige rekords of dele van `n rekord weerhou is kragtens een van die nege uitsonderings in die Freedom of Information Act. Dit is nie `n besluit oor jou vlugtelingaansoek nie.

2. Beteken dit my saak is gedeeltelik afgekeur?

Nee. Die weiering verwys slegs na die vrystelling van dokumente in reaksie op jou FOIA-versoek. Jou immigrasie- of vlugtelingstatus is apart van die FOIA-proses.

3. Wat is die langtermyngevolge van `n ongeskiktheidsbrief?

`n Ongeskiktheidsbevinding in USRAP is nie outomaties `n globale verbod op toekomstige aansoeke nie. Ander lande maak hul eie besluite onder hul eie wette. `n Onaangevegde ongeskiktheid kan egter toekomstige visum- of vlugtelingaansoeke bemoeilik. ARAI beveel sterk aan dat elke aansoeker `n CIS Ombudsman-saakhersiening indien en die besluit dokumenteer as deel van die sistemiese klagte. `n Formele uitdaging op rekord is die doeltreffendste verdediging.

4. Die vertroulikheidskennisgewing maak voorsiening vir deling met vreemde regerings – wat beteken dit in die praktyk?

Die USRAP-kennisgewing oor vertroulikheid (Augustus 2024) bepaal uitdruklik dat as jou aansoek afgekeur word, jou inligting met vreemde regerings gedeel kan word vir die doel van hervestiging in `n ander land. Dit bepaal ook dat die kennisgewing geen wetlik afdwingbare regte skep nie. Dit is `n ernstige privaatheidskwessie, en ARAI dokumenteer hierdie klousule as deel van sy sistemiese klagte.

5. Hoe dien ek `n formele klagte in oor die hantering van my saak?

Jy het verskeie kanale: (1) Die CIS Ombudsman – dien `n saakhersieningsversoek in by dhs.gov; (2) DHS OIG – dien `n klagte in oor bedrog, vermorsing, misbruik of wangedrag by oig.dhs.gov; (3) `n Privacy Act-versoek aan USCIS as jy glo jou data is misbruik. ARAI het sjablone vir elk van hierdie.

6. Wat is die CIS Ombudsman en watter gesag het hulle?

Die CIS Ombudsman is `n onafhanklike DHS-kantoor wat individue bystaan om probleme met USCIS op te los. Hulle kan nie `n besluit omkeer nie, maar hulle kan USCIS dwing om `n saak te herevalueer, foute reg te stel en antwoorde te verskaf. Kragtens 6 U.S.C. § 272 is hulle gemagtig om sistemiese probleempatrone te ondersoek – presies wat ARAI hulle vra om te doen.

7. Is daar `n appèlproses vir `n ongeskiktheidsbrief?

In die meeste USRAP-gevalle is daar geen formele appèlproses nie. Jy kan egter: (1) `n CIS Ombudsman-saakhersiening aanvra; (2) `n FOIA-versoek indien vir die volledige rekord; (3) `n immigrasieprokureur raadpleeg oor moontlike heropening; en (4) die afkeuring dokumenteer as deel van ARAI se sistemiese patroonklagte. Elke gedokumenteerde saak versterk die kollektiewe saak.

8. Hoe lank neem `n FOIA-versoek?

USCIS het 20 werksdae om op `n FOIA-versoek te antwoord, maar vertragings is algemeen en sommige versoeke neem maande. `n "Partial denial" beteken dat sommige rekords weerhou is kragtens `n wettige uitsondering. ARAI verskaf `n standaard FOIA-sjabloon vir aansoekers.

9. Wat as USCIS my FOIA-versoek heeltemal ignoreer?

As USCIS nie binne die statutêre sperdatum antwoord nie, kan jy: (1) appelleer by die USCIS FOIA-Appèlkantoor; (2) `n geding in die federale distrikshof instel; (3) `n klagte by die DHS OIG indien. Stilte is nie `n wettige antwoord nie – dit is `n skending van die FOIA-statute, en ARAI dokumenteer hierdie versuime.

10. Wat is CWS/RSC en watter gesag het hulle oor my data?

CWS/RSC (Resettlement Support Centers) is kontrakteurs wat USRAP-sake namens die Amerikaanse regering verwerk. Hulle moet die Privacy Act en USCIS-dataooreenkomste nakom. Hulle het nie onafhanklike gesag oor jou data nie. As jy glo jou data is misbruik of onbehoorlik gedeel, kan jy `n Privacy Act-versoek by USCIS indien en `n klagte by DHS OIG.

Sources: DOJ FOIA Reference Guide; CIS Ombudsman FOIA Teleconference (2010); USRAP Notice on Confidentiality (Aug 2024); 6 U.S.C. § 272. This is for informational purposes and does not constitute legal advice.
