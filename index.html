Official Declaration of Independence

Amptelike Verklaring van Onafhanklikheid

Afrikaans

Die Afrikaner Regte-Advokasie-inisiatief (ARAI) bevestig hiermee dat ons geen verbintenis, vennootskap of affiliasie met enige eksterne organisasies, insluitend Safrikaner Compass, het nie. Ons is deur organisasies gekontak met eise dat ons ons onafhanklike forensiese oudit staak, verwysings na openbare dokumente verwyder, en ons onderhewig stel aan hul "toesig."

1. Die Agenda van Hekwagters

Dit is duidelik dat sekere inisiatiewe nie veg vir die aansoeker nie, maar veg vir 'n magsbasis. Deur beheer oor kommunikasie te eis, direkte kontak met regeringsamptenare te verbied, en hulself as die enigste "hekwagters" van die USRAP-pyplyn te posisioneer, poog hierdie groepe om die aansoeker-populasie in 'n posisie van afhanklikheid te hou. Hul doel is om die administratiewe agteruitgang te bestuur, nie om die onreg te stop nie.

2. Integriteit teenoor Intimidasie

ARAI weier om geïntimideer te word deur dreigemente of eise vir ons operasionele besonderhede. Ons sal nie die identiteite van ons oudit-bydraers blootstel aan organisasies wat meer besorg is oor hul publieke beeld as oor die regte van vervolgde Afrikaners nie. Indien hierdie groepe nie die fundamentele regtelike onderskeid tussen 'n statutêre reg tot prosedurele billikheid en 'n owerheids-verleende voorreg kan begryp nie, het hulle geen plek in die leiding van hierdie oudit nie.

3. Ons Verbintenis

Ons dokumenteer hierdie sistemiese mislukking sedert Maart 2026. Ons sal nie terugdeins vir diegene wat eers by die stryd aangesluit het nadat dit 'n winsgewende of gewilde saak geword het nie. ARAI bly 'n onafhanklike, open-source ouditprojek. Ons is verantwoordbaar aan die aansoekers wat ons dien, nie aan die hekwagters wat hul toegang tot geregtigheid wil monopoliseer nie.

English

The Afrikaner Rights Advocacy Initiative (ARAI) hereby confirms that we have no affiliation, partnership, or coordination with any external organizations, including Safrikaner Compass. We have been approached by organizations demanding that we cease our independent forensic work, remove references to public documentation, and subject ourselves to their "oversight."

1. The Gatekeeper Agenda

It has become evident that certain initiatives are not fighting for the applicant—they are fighting for a power base. By demanding control over messaging, prohibiting direct contact with government officials, and positioning themselves as the sole "gatekeepers" of the USRAP pipeline, these groups maintain control over the applicant population. Their goal is to manage the decline, not to stop the injustice.

2. Integrity vs. Intimidation

ARAI refuses to be intimidated by threats or demands for our operational details. We will not disclose the identities of our audit contributors to organizations that have proven themselves more concerned with their public image than with the legal rights of persecuted Afrikaners. If these groups cannot grasp the fundamental legal distinction between a statutory right to procedural due process and a government-granted privilege, they have no place leading this audit.

3. Our Commitment

We have been documenting this systemic failure since March 2026. We will not back down to those who "jumped on the bandwagon" only after it became a profitable or popular cause. ARAI remains an independent, open-source audit project. We answer to the applicants we serve, not to the gatekeepers who seek to monopolize their access to justice.

No further correspondence will be entered into with external initiatives.

Geen verdere korrespondensie word met eksterne inisiatiewe gevoer nie.

Data Custodian's Declaration

In late June and July 2026, ARAI first documented a series of anomalies in the Afrikaner USRAP adjudication pathway: 2025 approvals followed by denials after an tremendous long waiting period, cancelled flight arrangements, contradictory case statuses, and correspondence routed through automated queues without individual adjudication.

We began our investigation because these irregularities did not fit the pattern of individual administrative error. They indicated systemic dysfunction.

That same pattern is now repeating itself. The question is not whether this should be documented – it is who will document it. If we do not, who will? If we do not act now, when? For whom must we wait to expose these irregularities and bring them into the light?

ARAI was established to answer that call. We are not waiting for permission. We are not waiting for approval. We are documenting the evidence, preserving the records, and preparing the case for formal submission to the appropriate oversight authorities.

The truth will be recorded, whether the system acknowledges it or not.

Verklaring van die Data-bewaarder

In laat Junie en Julie 2026 het ARAI vir die eerste keer 'n reeks anomalieë in die Afrikaner USRAP-adjudikasieroete gedokumenteer: 2025 goedkeurings gevolg deur afwysings na 'n geweldige lang wagtyd, gekanselleerde vlugreëlings, teenstrydige saakstatusse, en korrespondensie wat deur outomatiese stelsels gestuur is sonder individuele adjudikasie.

Ons het ons ondersoek begin omdat hierdie onreëlmatighede nie die patroon van individuele administratiewe foute gevolg het nie. Hulle het sistemiese wanfunksionering aangedui.

Daardie selfde patroon herhaal hom nou. Die vraag is nie of dit gedokumenteer moet word nie – dit is wie dit sal dokumenteer. As ons dit nie doen nie, wie sal? As ons nie nou optree nie, wanneer? Vir wie moet ons wag om hierdie onreëlmatighede bloot te lê en in die lig te bring?

ARAI is gestig om daardie oproep te beantwoord. Ons wag nie vir toestemming nie. Ons wag nie vir goedkeuring nie. Ons dokumenteer die bewyse, bewaar die rekords, en berei die saak voor vir formele voorlegging aan die toepaslike toesighoudende owerhede.

Die waarheid sal opgeteken word, of die stelsel dit erken of nie.

— ARAI Data Custodian / ARAI Data-bewaarder

Audit Mission / Oudit Missie

ARAI conducts independent forensic audits of USRAP adjudications to expose systemic corruption and administrative malpractice. Our audit focuses on:

  • Systemic Rep 16 Denials: Investigating automated, bad-faith batch denials.
  • Procedural Negligence: Documenting the impact of repeated medicals, fingerprinting, and security checks.
  • Due Process Violations: Addressing adversarial interviews, coercion, and intimidation tactics.
  • CIS Ombudsman Advocacy: Providing standardized tools for formal complaints against administrative failures.

ARAI voer onafhanklike forensiese oudits uit op USRAP-adjudikasies om sistemiese korrupsie en administratiewe wanpraktyke bloot te lê. Ons fokus op:

  • Sistemiese Rep 16-weierings: Ondersoek van geoutomatiseerde, kwade trou batch-weierings.
  • Prosedurele Nalatigheid: Dokumentering van herhaaldelike mediese toetse, vingerafdrukke en sekuriteitsondersoeke.
  • Skending van Regverdige Proses: Adressering van teenstrydige (adversarial) onderhoude, dwang en intimidasie.
  • CIS Ombudsman-voorspraak: Voorsiening van gestandaardiseerde gereedskap vir formele klagtes teen administratiewe foute.
