Security Advisory

⚠️ INTEGRITY ALERT
The ARAI portal utilizes cryptographic hashing for all evidence. Unauthorized scripts, bots, or automated scrapers interfere with our audit integrity and violate federal digital security standards.

To ensure the integrity of the Afrikaner Rights Advocacy Initiative’s forensic data, we prohibit the use of unauthorized automated tools.

What is permitted: Manual review, legitimate inquiries, and submission of evidence via the Custodian’s secure protocols.

What is prohibited: Automated scrapers, line-jumping scripts, and attempts to access the portal via deceptive identity spoofing.
