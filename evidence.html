ARAI Forensic Call Protocol

Note: This protocol is designed to elicit information under the Privacy Act of 1974 (5 U.S.C. § 552a). You have the right to access records pertaining to your own file.

1. The Script (Use this exactly)

"I am calling to verify the status of my administrative record for data integrity purposes. My A-Number is [A#]. I am requesting verification of the certifying official for my case file."

2. Advanced Data Integrity Prompts

If you encounter "Rep 16" or "Blank Screens," use these prompts:

  • On Batch Processing: "My correspondence was issued by 'Rep 16'. Can you verify if this is an individual adjudicator or an automated batch process? I request the official’s name for my file."
  • On System Errors: "My account shows a blank screen/null data. Please trigger a manual sync of my CLAIMS database entry. This discrepancy suggests a backend synchronization error."

3. Regulatory Foundation (Your Legal Authority)

Source 1: Privacy Act of 1974 (5 U.S.C. § 552a): Grants individuals the right to access records maintained by federal agencies and ensure the accuracy of said records.
Source 2: USCIS Policy Manual (Vol. 1, Part A, Chapter 3): Establishes the right of benefit requestors to obtain information about their cases through official channels.
Source 3: INA § 207: Governs the refugee status adjudication process and the requirement for individual assessment of claims.
