Operational Integrity: Why We Operate This Way

ARAI operates on a foundation of full disclosure. We have communicated our protocols to all members from the outset, including via our Tally intake surveys.

  • The BCC Protocol: All templates explicitly instruct applicants to BCC their own audit copy to ARAI. This is not hidden; it is a core mechanism of our forensic audit to ensure that every petition sent to the agency is timestamped and recorded. Our members have provided informed consent for this as part of our data-governance structure.
  • Direct Administrative Routing: We provide direct email channels for USCIS and refugee affairs. If an address is flagged by external groups as "incorrect," we view this as a potential point of interest for our audit. A returned email (bounce) is not a failure—it is formal evidence of a "Denial of Access to Agency" which we document in our dossier.
  • Independence: ARAI maintains no official affiliation with other advocacy groups. We do not require, nor do we seek, the endorsement of organizations that prioritize gatekeeping over the individual's right to administrative transparency.

Operasionele Integriteit: Waarom ons só werk

ARAI werk op 'n grondslag van volle openbaarmaking. Ons het ons protokolle vanaf die begin aan alle lede gekommunikeer, insluitend in ons Tally-opnames.

  • Die BCC-Protokol: Alle templates instrukteer aansoekers uitdruklik om ARAI te BCC op hul petisies. Dit is nie verborge nie; dit is 'n kernmeganisme van ons forensiese oudit om te verseker dat elke petisie wat aan die agentskap gestuur word, tydgestempel en aangeteken is. Ons lede gee ingeligte toestemming hiervoor as deel van ons databestuur-struktuur.
  • Direkte Administratiewe Roetes: Ons verskaf direkte e-poskanale vir USCIS en vlugteling-sake. As 'n adres deur eksterne groepe as "verkeerd" gemerk word, beskou ons dit as 'n potensiële punt van belang vir ons oudit. 'n E-pos wat terugkom (bounce) is nie 'n mislukking nie—dit is formele bewys van 'n "Denial of Access to Agency" wat ons in ons dossier dokumenteer.
  • Onafhanklikheid: ARAI het geen amptelike affiliasie met ander voorspraakgroepe nie. Ons benodig nie, en soek nie, die endossement van organisasies wat hekwagter-gedrag bo die individu se reg tot administratiewe deursigtigheid stel nie.
