1. Email Header Analysis / E-pos Header Analise

Gmail: Three dots (⋮) -> "Show Original".

Outlook: Right-click -> "View" -> "Message Details".

Target: Locate "Message-ID" and "AuthSource" to verify batch timestamps.

2. USCIS Forensic Call Protocol

Timing: 14:30 – 16:00 (SAST) / 08:30 – 10:00 (EST)

Key Inquiries:

  • "Can I have your Agent ID and Call Reference Number?"
  • "Can you confirm the last recorded action date on Receipt [NUMBER]?"
  • "Is this file with a human officer or a processing unit?"
  • "Can you confirm the internal 'Reason Code' for the last status change?"

3. FOIA Integrity & Privacy

Rule: Never share your FOIA records with unverified third parties.

Action: Submit findings directly to the CIS Ombudsman. Use the Ombudsman portal to track your individual case progress.
