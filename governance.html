ARAI: GOVERNANCE, STANDARDS, & CONDUCT

Our Vision

To see a system-wide administrative rectification for Afrikaner refugee applicants who have been systematically let down. We strive to bring the reality of our community to the attention of the correct oversight bodies, replacing panic and reactive chaos with disciplined, unified action.

Our Mission

To serve as the Data Custodian for a community of resilient individuals. We ensure that every applicant is treated as a person with a valued story, whose input is vital. We document systemic malpractice to bring about change for all Afrikaners, ensuring that our collective voice reaches the right oversight committees through high-standard, forensic work.

Our Core Values

  • The Dignity of the Individual: Your case is not a statistic. Your story has immense value—it is the evidence that drives our mission.
  • Disciplined Patience: We move with the calculated precision of those who know that forensic investigations achieve more than raw emotion.
  • Constructive Contribution: We foster a culture where volunteers help, document anomalies, and contribute to the collective goal.
  • Institutional Integrity: We bring human impact statements into alignment with statutory evidence. We report malpractice with a dossier based on a balance of evidence.

Code of Practice: Compliance & Truth

  • Adherence to Refugee Status: We adhere to the 1951 Refugee Convention, 1967 Protocol, and INA § 101(a)(42). We focus on protected grounds and objective persecution.
  • The Affidavit of Truth: Every submission is governed by 28 U.S.C. § 1746. We mandate total transparency and accuracy.
  • Procedural Conformity: We strictly align with USCIS RAIO training modules, the Refugee Processing Manual (RPM), and the USCIS Policy Manual.
  • Directed Oversight Engagement: We engage the White House, House/Senate Judiciary Committees, CIS Ombudsman, and OIG using technical submissions that highlight the gap between executive policy directives and field-level malpractice.
⚠️ URGENT SECURITY ADVISORY: The “Bot” Trap
Do not use unauthorized browser extensions or “hacking” scripts (e.g., Tampermonkey). They provide false status data and risk a permanent bar for fraud (INA §212(a)(6)(i)). ARAI only uses manual inspection. Maintain a “Clean Room” environment.
