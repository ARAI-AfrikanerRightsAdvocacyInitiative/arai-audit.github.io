Legal Foundation

Our operations are guided by the policy of the United States as established in the Presidential Action of February 7, 2025, concerning humanitarian relief for persecuted Afrikaners. We adhere to the administrative record standards set by the Administrative Procedure Act (APA) § 706 and INA § 207.
