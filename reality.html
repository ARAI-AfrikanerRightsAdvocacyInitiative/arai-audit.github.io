Administrative Reality / Administratiewe Realiteit

English

You Are Not Failing, The System Is.

We observe a recurring pattern of "gaslighting" where applicants are told that contradictions and reversals are "normal." The forensic reality is:

  • Approval-to-Denial is not normal: It is a systemic breakdown between USCIS adjudicators and RSC/CWS logistics.
  • "MyCase" is unreliable: Status discrepancies are evidence of database corruption, not your failure.
  • Your rights are not conditional: USCIS is bound by the Administrative Procedure Act (APA). They cannot arbitrarily reverse decisions without cause.
  • Do not accept the blame: Those who defend these failures are protecting a broken system, not you.

Afrikaans

Die Stelsel Faal, Nie Jy Nie.

Ons sien 'n herhalende patroon waar mense vertel word dat teenstrydighede en afwysings "normaal" is. Die forensiese realiteit is:

  • "Goedkeuring-na-Afwysing" is nie normaal nie: Dit is 'n sistemiese ineenstorting tussen USCIS-besluitnemers en RSC/CWS-logistiek.
  • "MyCase" is onbetroubaar: Status-teenstrydighede is bewys van databasis-korrupsie, nie jou onvermoë nie.
  • Jou regte is nie voorwaardelik nie: USCIS is gebind deur die Administrative Procedure Act (APA). Hulle mag nie besluite willekeurig omkeer nie.
  • Moenie die blaam aanvaar nie: Diegene wat hierdie foute verdedig, beskerm 'n gebrekkige stelsel – nie jou belange nie.

ARAI’s position: We do not accept institutional negligence as standard procedure. Knowledge is your only harness.
