Administrative Reality / Administratiewe Realiteit

English

Statement by ARAI Data Custodian

The refugee program is not a favour or a privilege. It is a legal duty owed to those fleeing persecution. The United States' obligations under international law do not dissolve because administrative systems are disorganised. You are not claiming a privilege; you are claiming protection to which you are entitled.

ARAI will not soften this reality: the systemic failures you are experiencing – approvals followed by denials, cancelled flights, contradictory statuses – are not your fault. They are evidence of administrative negligence. Those who dismiss your legitimate grievances as "unrealistic expectations" are not protecting you. They are protecting a broken system.

Do not vent where it can be used against you. Social media is a breeding ground for gaslighting and misinformation. Seek out vetted, credible sources.

Empower yourself. File your own FOIA. Check your own headers. Build your own case from the evidence. Knowledge is your power and strength; it is the only insurance that cannot be cancelled.

— ARAI Data Custodian

Afrikaans

Verklaring deur ARAI Data-bewaarder

Die vlugtelingprogram is nie 'n guns of 'n gawe of 'n voorreg nie. Dit is 'n wetlike plig teenoor diegene wat voor vervolging vlug. Die Verenigde State se verpligtinge onder internasionale reg verdwyn nie omdat administratiewe stelsels wanfunksioneer nie. Jy eis nie 'n voorreg nie; jy eis beskerming waarop jy geregtig is.

ARAI sal nie hierdie realiteit versag nie: die sistemiese falings wat jy ervaar – goedkeurings gevolg deur afwysings, gekanselleerde vlugte, teenstrydige statusse – is nie jou skuld nie. Dit is getuienis van administratiewe nalatigheid. Diegene wat jou regmatige klagtes as "onrealistiese verwagtings" afmaak, beskerm nie jou nie. Hulle beskerm 'n gebreekte stelsel.

Moenie uitvaar en afpak waar dit teen jou gebruik kan word nie. Sosiale media is 'n broeiplek vir gaslighting en waninligting. Soek betroubare, geverifieerde bronne.

Bemagtig jouself. Dien jou eie FOIA in. Kontroleer jou eie headers. Bou jou eie saak uit die bewyse. Kennis is jou krag en mag; dit is die enigste versekering wat nie gekanselleer kan word nie.

— ARAI Data-bewaarder
