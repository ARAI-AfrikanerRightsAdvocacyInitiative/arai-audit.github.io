Administrative Reality / Administratiewe Realiteit

English

Statement by ARAI Data Custodian

The refugee program is not a favour or a privilege. It is a legal duty owed to those fleeing persecution. The United States' obligations under international law do not dissolve because administrative systems are disorganised. You are not claiming a privilege; you are claiming protection to which you are entitled.

ARAI will not soften this reality: the systemic failures you are experiencing – approvals followed by denials, cancelled flights, contradictory statuses – are not your fault. They are evidence of administrative negligence. Those who dismiss your legitimate grievances as "unrealistic expectations" are not protecting you. They are protecting a broken system.

Do not vent where it can be used against you. Social media is a breeding ground for gaslighting and misinformation. Seek out vetted, credible sources.

Empower yourself. File your own FOIA. Check your own headers. Build your own case from the evidence. Knowledge is your power and strength; it is the only insurance that cannot be cancelled.

— ARAI Data Custodian

Dit is getuienis van administratiewe nalatigheid. Diegene wat jou regmatige klagtes as "onrealistiese verwagtings" afmaak, beskerm nie jou nie. Hulle beskerm 'n gebreekte stelsel.

The Myth of Hidden Information / Die Mite van Verborge Inligting

English

When applicants are denied after approval, group members often speculate: "Perhaps you hid something." This is not fact; it is guesswork designed to shift blame onto the applicant.

USCIS does not need your social media posts to know your background. They have access to international databases, biometrics, and interagency records. If you were ineligible, they would have denied you before issuing an approval letter.

A denial after approval – without a specific reason – is evidence of a systemic failure, not hidden information. The only way to know the actual basis of a decision is to file a FOIA request for your A-File and examine the adjudicator's notes yourself.

Do not accept blame without evidence. Do not let speculation silence you. Empower yourself with your own records.

Afrikaans

Wanneer aansoekers ná goedkeuring afgewys word, spekuleer groeplede dikwels: "Miskien het jy iets weggesteek." Dit is nie 'n feit nie; dit is raaiwerk wat ontwerp is om die skuld op die aansoeker te plaas.

USCIS het nie jou sosiale media-plasings nodig om jou agtergrond te ken nie. Hulle het toegang tot internasionale databasisse, biometrie en interagentskap-rekords. As jy onbevoeg was, sou hulle jou afgekeur het voordat hulle 'n goedkeuringsbrief uitgereik het.

'n Afwysing ná goedkeuring – sonder 'n spesifieke rede – is bewys van 'n sistemiese faling, nie verborge inligting nie. Die enigste manier om die werklike basis van 'n besluit te ken, is om 'n FOIA-versoek vir jou A-lêer in te dien en die adjudikeerder se notas self te ondersoek.

Moenie blaam aanvaar sonder bewyse nie. Moenie toelaat dat spekulasie jou stilmaak nie. Bemagtig jouself met jou eie rekords.

Family Units & Legal Status / Gesinseenhede en Wettige Status

English

When one member of a hard-linked household is approved and another denied, many group members rush to advise: "Get married." This advice is dangerous.

Marriage entered into primarily for immigration benefit can be treated as fraud by USCIS. It may jeopardize the approved applicant's status and create criminal exposure for both parties.

A split decision on a hard-linked case is not a sign that you should change your relationship status. It is evidence of an arbitrary, inconsistent adjudication process. The same household, the same interviews, the same medicals – different outcomes – demonstrates a lack of individual review.

If you face this situation, do not make life-altering decisions based on social media advice. Document the inconsistency, keep your denial letter, keep proof of your linked application, and consult a licensed US immigration attorney before acting.

Afrikaans

Wanneer een lid van 'n hardgekoppelde huishouding goedgekeur word en 'n ander afgekeur word, raai baie groeplede dadelik: "Trou net." Hierdie raad is gevaarlik.

'n Huwelik wat hoofsaaklik vir immigrasievoordeel aangegaan word, kan deur USCIS as bedrog behandel word. Dit kan die goedgekeurde aansoeker se status in gevaar stel en kriminele blootstelling vir beide partye skep.

'n Verdeelde besluit op 'n hardgekoppelde saak is nie 'n teken dat jy jou verhoudingstatus moet verander nie. Dit is bewys van 'n arbitrêre, teenstrydige adjudikasieproses. Dieselfde huishouding, dieselfde onderhoude, dieselfde mediese – verskillende uitkomste – toon 'n gebrek aan individuele hersiening.

As jy hierdie situasie in die gesig staar, moenie lewensveranderende besluite neem op grond van sosiale media-raad nie. Dokumenteer die teenstrydigheid, bewaar jou afkeurbrief, bewaar bewys van jou gekoppelde aansoek, en raadpleeg eers 'n gelisensieerde Amerikaanse immigrasieprokureur.
